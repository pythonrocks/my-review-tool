How it works

myReview uses your gh login to list pull requests, read diffs and post reviews as you. It runs claude or codex on this Mac, read-only or sandboxed, on a checkout of the pull request. myReview itself sends nothing anywhere else, and its data stays in ~/Library/Application Support/myReview.
